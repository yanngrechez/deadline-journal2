ABOUT

Politics from the people closest to it.

Deadline Journal is a student-led international publication built around a simple idea: the people closest to a country often know which stories outsiders are missing.

Our contributors write about their home countries, communities, institutions and histories, bringing local context to global conversations.

The publication is organized by region, while articles can span politics, history and economics.
